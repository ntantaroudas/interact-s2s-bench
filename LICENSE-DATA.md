# Licence of the data files

The code of this repository is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). The data files of the study are under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/:

* `runs_cpu/` and `runs_cpu_repeat/`, the run files of the streaming experiment (released words with their
  recognition and release times, and computing times),
* `results/`, the per-meeting and summary tables of all measures, the statistical tests, the results of the
  perfect-transcript analysis and the data behind every figure,
* `data/lexicon.json`, the metadata of the International Sign dictionary (entry keys, frame counts and spans
  with visible hands, without landmark coordinates),
* `manuscript/figures_v4/`, the figures of the article.

The run files and results contain automatic transcripts of recordings of the AMI Meeting Corpus, which is

J. Carletta et al., The AMI Meeting Corpus: A Pre-announcement, in Machine Learning for Multimodal
Interaction (MLMI 2005), Springer, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1007/11677482_3. The dictionary metadata are
derived from N. D. Tantaroudas, International Sign extractions and data AVATAR animations, Zenodo, 2026,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18656296.
